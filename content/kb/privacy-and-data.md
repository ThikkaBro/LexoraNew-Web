# Privacy and data handling on this website

This mirrors the published privacy policy at /privacy. It is here because that
page is written as JSX rather than living in `app/site-config.ts`, so the
ingestion script cannot read it directly. Keep the two in step.

## Does this website track visitors?

Keywords: privacy, tracking, cookies, analytics, gdpr, data, personal data, store, stored, storing, retention

No. The site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics and no advertising
scripts, and cannot identify a visitor from a normal page view. The hosting
provider, Vercel, keeps standard server logs including IP address, browser type
and requested URL, in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse.

## What does the assistant panel collect?

Keywords: chatbot, assistant, chat, data, store, stored, storing, save, saved, record, transcript, conversation, privacy, confidential, secure

Nothing until a visitor types a message and sends it. At that point the message
and the conversation so far go to the server and on to the AI provider that
generates the reply. The conversation is kept in the visitor's own browser for
the length of the visit and is gone when the tab closes. Transcripts are not
stored on the server. Token counts are recorded so the running cost is visible,
and those records contain no message content. IP addresses are used to count
requests and prevent abuse, and are not linked to anything else. Visitors should
not type anything confidential into it.

## What happens to a name and email left in the assistant?

Keywords: email, name, contact, details, lead, spam, marketing, sell, delete

They are sent to LexoraTech and nowhere else, along with the recent part of the
conversation so whoever replies has the context. They are used only to answer
the enquiry, never sold, and never added to a marketing list. They are deleted
on request.

## Who owns data during a client project?

Keywords: dpa, data processing agreement, controller, processor, client data, nda, confidential

During a project LexoraTech may be given access to systems containing personal
data belonging to the client's business. In that relationship the client is the
data controller and LexoraTech acts as a processor on the client's written
instructions. LexoraTech can sign a data processing agreement, and does not
retain copies of client data after handover unless asked to.

## What rights do UK and EU visitors have?

Keywords: gdpr, rights, erasure, delete, access request, subject access, ico, dpa

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, visitors can ask what personal data is held about
them, have it corrected or erased, and object to how it is used. Email
hello@lexoratech.com and LexoraTech will respond within 30 days. Visitors may
also complain to their national data protection authority.
